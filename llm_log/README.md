(Section 12 of the project statement). The whole team (Jose Azzi, Dia
Hajjar, Theresa Kozah) worked in joint sessions, so each conversation
was a team interaction. It was typed on the account or machine listed
below.

### Known limitation of the share pages

The PDFs contain every prompt and uploaded screenshot, but ChatGPT's
share pages do not show all of the assistant's answers:

- In chat 1, 32 answers appear as "This shared answer couldn't be
  displayed".
- In chats 2–4, only some answers are rendered.

The complete prompts and responses are in the ChatGPT accounts. They
can be exported with Settings → Data controls → Export data. The
exported files should be added to this folder.
